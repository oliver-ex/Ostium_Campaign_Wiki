# Daggerheart Campaign Conventions

This vault is structured for **Daggerheart**, using the terminology of the Daggerheart SRD v2.0 (August 25, 2026) as the reference point.

Official SRD: https://www.daggerheart.com/srd/

## PCs

Player-character pages can track:

- ancestry;
- community;
- class and subclass;
- level and domains;
- Agility, Strength, Finesse, Instinct, Presence, and Knowledge;
- Experiences;
- Evasion;
- Armor Score / Armor Slots;
- Major and Severe damage thresholds;
- Hit Points;
- Stress;
- Hope;
- class and Hope features;
- domain-card loadout and vault;
- weapons, armor, inventory, and gold;
- optional transformation or class-specific information.

The wiki is a campaign record, not a replacement for official character cards/sheets. Players can keep the page as detailed or lightweight as they prefer.

## NPCs

NPCs should not automatically be built like PCs.

When the GM needs mechanics, the private NPC page contains an adversary-style block with:

- Tier;
- adversary Type;
- Motives & Tactics;
- Difficulty;
- Major / Severe thresholds;
- HP;
- Stress;
- Attack Modifier;
- Standard Attack;
- optional Experiences;
- Features;
- Fear Features.

Narrative NPCs can remain statless until mechanics are useful.

## Locations

Private location pages can contain a Daggerheart Environment block:

- Tier;
- Type: Exploration, Social, Traversal, or Event;
- Description;
- Impulses;
- Difficulty;
- Potential Adversaries;
- Features;
- Feature Questions.

A location can support multiple different environments. The block in a location note is prep, not a rule that every visit must use the same mechanics.

## Campaign Acts and Tier

The three Ostium acts are story structure, not fixed Daggerheart tiers. Scale adversaries and environments to the party's actual level/tier when those scenes occur.

## Rot

No final mechanical Rot infection track has been established in this vault. Until one is deliberately designed:

- use Rot primarily as fictional positioning, environmental pressure, Stress, perception, and consequence;
- avoid arbitrary permanent character changes from a single bad roll;
- distinguish ordinary illness/trauma from supernatural infection;
- keep the cognitive effects strange without using mental illness as a synonym for evil or monstrosity.

## Homebrew Status

Any adversary or environment statistics written in this vault are original campaign prep and should be treated as adjustable homebrew, not official Daggerheart material.
