# New Ostium Government

<!-- page-summary:start -->

The civic authority preserving New Ostium’s stability while resisting obligations to the old city that it fears it cannot sustain.

<!-- page-summary:end -->


## At a Glance

| Pressure | What matters in play |
| --- | --- |
| True objective | Preserve a functioning city under genuine resource constraints without being dragged back into an unlimited obligation for Old Ostium. |
| Immediate pressure | Old Ostium crises repeatedly cross the wall through water, refugees, smuggling, and political pressure. |
| What they need | Predictable infrastructure, public legitimacy, housing capacity, and controlled cooperation. |
| What they fear | A failure that causes New Ostium residents to believe the government sacrificed their stability for an unsolvable old-city crisis. |
| How they might use the party | Formal commissions, negotiation, permits, rescue authorizations, investigation, and infrastructure intervention. |

## Relevant Roles to Populate Later

- Waterworks Liaison — coordinates with Old Ostium Engineers.
- Refugee Registrar — handles admission and temporary status.
- Museum Curator — potential stakeholder in [[Storylines/Adventures/The Heist|The Heist]].
- Emergency Planner — models cross-wall infrastructure failures.

## Actual Agenda

- Solve Pump 7 without accepting an open-ended responsibility for Old Ostium.
- Contain Shattered Axe incursions.
- Keep refugee policy politically sustainable.
- Enter negotiations if interdependence becomes undeniable.

## Secrets

- Many officials privately understand the wall cannot fully separate the cities.
- Capacity limits are real; 'the government is evil' should not be the solution to its choices.

## Resources

- Law, bureaucracy, guards, records, public works, tax capacity.

## Internal Tensions

- Security officials favour hard separation.
- Civic planners are more willing to cooperate because infrastructure forces them to.
- Politicians fear both humanitarian scandal and resource backlash.

## Relationships & Leverage

| Faction | Relationship / leverage |
| --- | --- |
| [[Factions/Old Ostium Engineers\|Old Ostium Engineers]] | Essential maintenance expertise makes material interdependence hard to deny. |

- [[Factions/Old Ostium Engineers|Old Ostium Engineers]] can embarrass claims of complete independence.
- The Merchants Guild can fund solutions but expects influence.
- [[Storylines/Adventures/Negotiations|Negotiations]] can create a new framework.

## Current Moves

- Prepare contingency plans around Pump 7.
- Increase scrutiny of cross-wall traffic after Shattered Axe incidents.
- Resist promises that lack resource plans.

## If the Party Helps

- The party can turn ad hoc cooperation into policy.

## If the Party Opposes

- The government can restrict access, prosecute crime, deny resources, or publicly discredit the party without becoming a combat antagonist.
