## At a Glance

**True objective:** Maintain enough organized force that Old Ostium's free settlements are not destroyed or absorbed by predatory powers.\
**Immediate pressure:** Protection costs more than settlements can comfortably pay.\
**What they need:** Food, equipment, medicine, replacement weapons, and enough legitimacy to keep collecting levies.\
**What they fear:** A supply collapse or corruption scandal that causes protected settlements to stop paying before an alternative exists.\
**How they might use the party:** Escort, anti-raider work, supply recovery, internal investigation, or political negotiation.\

## Leadership Hierarchy

1. [[Characters/NPCs/Indra Karaf|Indra Karaf]] — Commander; strategic and moral authority.
2. [[Characters/NPCs/Calev Marr|Calev Marr]] — Quartermaster; controls logistics and levy accounting.
3. **Sari Pell** — Patrol Captain; coordinates district patrols and rapid response.
4. **Tomas Varek** — Adjudicator; handles disputes involving Protectorate contracts and misconduct.

## Relevant Roles to Populate Later

- Scout Lead — tracks raider movements and unsafe streets.
- Levy Collector — politically sensitive face of payment enforcement.
- Training Sergeant — integrates volunteers and reformed criminals.
- Settlement Liaison — negotiates terms with protected communities.

## Actual Agenda

- Hold the defensive line.
- Break major Shattered Axe raiding capacity.
- Secure outside supplies without becoming subordinate to New Ostium.

## Secrets

- The faction's costs are genuinely high.
- Some individual officers exploit their authority, but Indra does not secretly intend a protection racket.
- Indra may tolerate grey compromises if the alternative is losing the force.

## Resources

- Armed personnel.
- Fortified HQ.
- Patrol intelligence.
- Reputation for actually showing up when contracted.

## Internal Tensions

- Veterans favour discipline; militia elements resist hierarchy.
- Former criminals and former police distrust each other.
- Settlement resentment of levies can become explosive.

## Relationships & Leverage

- [[Factions/Old Ostium Hospital District Community|Old Ostium Hospital District community]] needs them but resents the price.
- [[Factions/The Shattered Axe|The Shattered Axe]] benefits if they look corrupt or weak.
- New Ostium may cooperate tactically while refusing to recognize them as legitimate authority.

## Current Moves

- Pressure the Shattered Axe.
- Seek supplies.
- Decide whether to expose internal abuse or contain it quietly.

## If the Party Helps

- The party can gain safe routes, armed backup, and political weight in Old Ostium.

## If the Party Opposes

- The Protectorate can become a serious obstacle, but conflict should still reflect that weakening it may expose civilians.
