# New Ostium Merchants Guild

<!-- page-summary:start -->

A powerful trade organization that stabilizes New Ostium’s supply chains while concentrating wealth and pursuing selective profit from the old city.

<!-- page-summary:end -->


## At a Glance

| Pressure | What matters in play |
| --- | --- |
| True objective | Keep concentrated control of trade while converting Old Ostium's danger into selective profit. |
| Immediate pressure | Smuggling, political suspicion, and increasing demand for resources tied to Old Ostium. |
| What they need | Stable law, protected warehouses, information, and profitable controlled access to salvage. |
| What they fear | Uncontrolled Old Ostium instability disrupting the trade system the Guild built. |
| How they might use the party | Fund retrievals, hire protection, request artifact work, or become a target the party must negotiate with or rob. |

## Relevant Roles to Populate Later

- Artifact Appraiser — authenticates Old Ostium salvage.
- Customs Fixer — resolves border and import problems.
- Warehouse Master — controls sensitive goods and access.
- Political Liaison — bargains with New Ostium officials.

## Actual Agenda

- Preserve monopoly-like advantages.
- Invest in Old Ostium only when risk can be externalized or controlled.
- Suppress theft that could make Guild security look weak.

## Secrets

- Several major fortunes were enlarged by exploiting forced sales and desperate competitors during relocation.
- The Guild can plausibly argue that consolidation also restored stable supply chains.

## Resources

- Money, credit, logistics, security, and political access.

## Internal Tensions

- Trine's appetite for strange acquisitions can conflict with more conservative members.
- Some members want deeper Old Ostium exploitation; others want complete disengagement.

## Current Moves

- Increase security around high-value goods.
- Identify promising Old Ostium investments.
- Pressure government for favourable rules.

## If the Party Helps

- The party gains money, equipment access, and political doors.

## If the Party Opposes

- The Guild can use law, credit, reputation, investigators, and security rather than simply sending assassins.
