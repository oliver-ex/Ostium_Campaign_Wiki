## Goals, Agenda & Pressures

The Guard's funding and excellent weapons make its authority a practical reality. Illegal business anywhere in New Ostium carries a high risk of investigation, arrest, and serious consequences. A sympathetic motive does not make theft or smuggling safe.

## Relationships & Internal Tensions

- [[Factions/New Ostium Government|New Ostium Government]] provides the civic context for law enforcement. The existing City Marshal, Ren Varo, remains in that government's security and border-enforcement role; the exact reporting arrangement with Aaron is **Unknown / TBD**.
- [[Factions/New Ostium Merchants Guild|New Ostium Merchants Guild]] benefits from secure trade. Private Guild security and the City Guard are distinct forces; wealth does not establish control over Aaron.
- **Pressure for play:** Order protects residents, but enforcement can also uphold the exclusions described in [[Locations/New Ostium|New Ostium]]. Individual disputes need evidence and context rather than a blanket moral verdict on the Guard.

## GM Guidance — Avoid Combat

> [!danger] Do not fight the City Guard
> The PCs should not fight this faction under any circumstances, especially at low levels. Outright combat in New Ostium is a bad idea: a local disturbance can bring a formidable, well-equipped force into play. Do not present patrols as level-appropriate enemies or a source of equipment to loot.

Make the danger clear before the party commits: describe excellent weapons, disciplined formations, and visible coordination. Explain the stakes directly when needed; this warning requires no check. The force is formidable, not omniscient: discovery still depends on witnesses, evidence, and what actually happens.

**Possible responses, not completed events:** Officers can demand a halt, separate participants, summon support, or make arrests as circumstances warrant. Give the party opportunities to de-escalate, cooperate, explain coercion, seek lawful authorization, negotiate, or withdraw before violence. If players choose violence despite warnings, resolve their actions honestly and carry forward the consequences; do not turn the Guard into a balanced combat encounter.

Apply this pressure to [[Storylines/Adventures/The Heist|The Heist]] and [[Storylines/Adventures/Heist II|Heist II]]. Illegal approaches remain possible choices, with severe risks; lawful bargains, exposing coercion, and alternative sources of resources remain meaningful options.

## Knowledge & Lore Status

- **Canon:** The Guard operates in New Ostium, is well funded, has excellent weapons, and is a formidable force led by Aaron Fortisax, a Dragonborn Paladin. Mira Vey is its lieutenant.
- **GM guidance:** Avoid confrontation at every level; especially at low levels, the Guard is an overwhelming deterrent. Illegal business is highly dangerous and open combat in the city is a bad idea.
- **PCs know:** No introduction or warning has been recorded in play. Deliver the danger clearly when it becomes relevant, then record what the PCs learned.
- **Unknown / TBD:** Headcount, budget, headquarters, precise legal penalties, and command relationships beyond Aaron and Mira.
