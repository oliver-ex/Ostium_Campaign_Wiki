# Old Ostium Hospital District — Community

<!-- page-summary:start -->

An autonomous community keeping patients and residents alive amid fragile water supplies, the Rot, shortages, and costly protection.

<!-- page-summary:end -->


## At a Glance

| Pressure | What matters in play |
| --- | --- |
| True objective | Remain a viable autonomous community rather than becoming a ward of a stronger faction or collapsing under scarcity. |
| Immediate pressure | Water fragility, Rot cases, supply shortages, and the Protectorate levy. |
| What they need | Reliable water, medicine, food, patrols, and technical continuity if Taran leaves. |
| What they fear | A single infrastructure or security failure that forces mass evacuation into a New Ostium that cannot absorb everyone. |
| How they might use the party | Secure supplies, escort specialists, reduce levy pressure, protect routes, or solve water failures. |

## Relevant Roles to Populate Later

- Night Watch Captain — coordinates local barricades between Protectorate patrols.
- Burial Keeper — manages the dead and dangerous Rot cases.
- Volunteer Coordinator — assigns new arrivals to care, repair, kitchens, and sanitation.
- Water Steward — local operator trained to take over more of Taran's daily work.

## Actual Agenda

- Survive the opening arc without losing Taran or the water system.
- Reduce the cost of protection without losing it.
- Build more independent technical capacity.

## Secrets

- A few leaders suspect Taran's Rot symptoms.
- Residents disagree about whether keeping him in Old Ostium is morally acceptable.

## Resources

- Medical infrastructure.
- Community legitimacy.
- Large pool of motivated but exhausted volunteers.

## Internal Tensions

- Some residents want closer Protectorate integration; others fear dependency.
- Some want to petition New Ostium; others consider that humiliating or futile.

## Relationships & Leverage

| Faction | Relationship / leverage |
| --- | --- |
| [[Factions/The Protectorate\|The Protectorate]] | Provides protection at a levy the settlement struggles to afford. |
| [[Factions/Old Ostium Engineers\|Old Ostium Engineers]] | Potential source of training and technical support for fragile water systems. |

- The settlement can offer legitimacy to anyone who genuinely helps it.
- Its suffering can become political leverage in [[Storylines/Adventures/Negotiations|Negotiations]].

## Current Moves

- Keep paying protection.
- Ask the Engineers for more training.
- Hide the severity of some shortages to avoid panic.

## If the Party Helps

- The party gains a home base, trusted witnesses, care, and a concrete constituency in Old Ostium.

## If the Party Opposes

- If abandoned or exploited, the party may lose one of the campaign's strongest moral anchors.
