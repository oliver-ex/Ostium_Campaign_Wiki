## Secrets

- Dalia helped use the relocation crisis to eliminate or absorb rival merchant organizations.
- She can justify many decisions as stabilizing trade, and often is partly right.
- The Guild's warehouses and accounts can become targets in [[Storylines/Adventures/Heist II|Heist II]].

## Motives

- Concentrated capital can act when fragmented traders cannot.
- Chaos is an opportunity only if controlled.
- Protect the Guild's privileged position.

## Hidden Alliances & Relationships

- [[Characters/NPCs/Trine Baradur|Trine Baradur]] is useful but not central to the inner leadership.

## Backstory

Dalia was already wealthy before the collapse. She distinguished herself by understanding that relocation would reorder the city's economy. The Guild's consolidation was both opportunistic and an effective way to restore large-scale trade.

## What They Know

- Merchant holdings and reserves.
- Government procurement.
- Which Old Ostium salvage categories are quietly becoming valuable.

## Current Plans

- Oppose uncontrolled smuggling.
- Make selective investments in Old Ostium if returns and security justify them.
- Protect the Guild from theft and political backlash.
