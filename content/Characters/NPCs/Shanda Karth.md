## At a Glance

**Species (D&D 2024):** Elf

- Apothecary in [[Locations/New Ostium|New Ostium]]. Holds **two vials** of [[Creatures and Afflictions/Frigid Woe|Frigid Woe]] antidote.
- Procured them for [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]], the wealthy father in [[Storylines/Adventures/The Prodigal Son|The Prodigal Son]]. His wife, [[Characters/NPCs/Tara Lusat|Tara Lusat]], died from the Rot of Old Ostium; one vial is reserved for each of his two sons.
- The extraordinarily expensive vials **belong to Egbert**. She cannot independently sell or transfer them, even for substantial money; Egbert can authorize a negotiated sale or other arrangement.
- Knows a supply recently arrived in [[Locations/Mistbreak|Mistbreak]]. Can potentially arrange a paid courier; direct travel is also possible.

## What They Know

- The antidote exists, her **two vials** are reserved, and Mistbreak is a concrete source to pursue. Vial count does not establish dosing.
- Exact courier price, travel time, stockpile custodian, dose requirements, and delivery guarantees: **Needs decision**.
- Do not imply either son has Frigid Woe; Egbert's reservation is preventative. Taran's established Rot condition is separate.

## Motives & Portrayal — Tentative

- Professional, guarded, protective of her client's interests; contractual responsibility rather than inherent malice.
- Sympathy can open assistance, introductions, and logistics; it does not automatically release reserved medicine.

## Branches

- **Money:** explain that the stock belongs to Egbert; offer to ask whether he will sell one or both. Quote sale/courier costs only once negotiated or set by the GM.
- **Sympathy / persuasion:** offer a courier plan or request permission to contact the benefactor.
- **Favor:** introduce the existing Prodigal Son opportunity if the PCs want it; preserve its objectives and negotiate compensation rather than invent a mandatory new errand.
- **Benefactor:** name Egbert and offer an introduction. He may negotiate medicine for help with Taran, substantial gold, collateral, or a replacement plan. Speaking with him is optional.
- **Theft:** adjudicate access and consequences from the fiction. Two stolen vials are not an established cure for the whole party.
- **Travel:** give the Mistbreak lead without requiring a successful check.

## Dialogue — Tentative Prompts

- “Mistbreak has received a supply. We can discuss a courier, or you can go yourselves.”
- “The Rot took his wife, Tara. He fears losing more of his family. I can introduce you, if you wish.”
