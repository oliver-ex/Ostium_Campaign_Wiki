## At a Glance

**Species (D&D 2024):** Goliath

**Role in campaign:** Opening moral dilemma; engineer who ties the party to the waterworks\
**Usual Location:** [[Locations/Old Ostium Hospital District|Old Ostium Hospital District]]\
**Current agenda:** Remain long enough to keep the settlement's water infrastructure functioning and train/secure enough support that leaving would not endanger everyone.\
**Disposition toward party:** Initially wary if the party arrives as his father's agents; warmer if they engage seriously with the settlement's needs.\
**Voice / portrayal:** Educated but practical; speaks quickly when discussing infrastructure and slows when talking about his father.\
**What changes their mind:** A credible plan that protects the Hospital District and gives him a realistic path to treatment.\

## Secrets

- Taran is already afflicted by the Rot, but it is in an early, possibly curable stage.
- Signs should be present but not immediately announced on first meeting: lapses, intrusive perceptions, unusual fatigue, or other subtle symptoms can accumulate.
- His engineering work is genuinely instrumental. If he simply leaves, the Hospital District may lose reliable drinking water very quickly.

## Motives

- Duty to the people who depend on him.
- Love for his father despite resisting him.
- Guilt about having resources others do not.
- Fear that leaving means choosing his own safety over many other lives.

## Hidden Alliances & Relationships

- [[Characters/NPCs/Brock Drungur|Brock Drungur]] recognizes Taran's technical usefulness and may recognize symptoms before the party does.
- The [[Factions/Old Ostium Engineers|Old Ostium Engineers]] and [[Factions/Old Ostium Hospital District Community|Old Ostium Hospital District — Community]] both depend on him more than Egbert understands.

## Family & Antidote Connection

- [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]] is his father; Taran has a brother whose name and circumstances are TBD.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Sara Lusat|Sara Lusat]] is his sister.
- Their mother, [[Characters/NPCs/Tara Lusat|Tara Lusat]], died from [[Creatures and Afflictions/The Rot|the Rot of Old Ostium]]. Egbert commissioned [[Characters/NPCs/Shanda Karth|Shanda Karth]] to hold two antidote vials, one for each son as a precaution.
- Taran's established affliction remains the **Rot**. He is not established as infected with Frigid Woe, and its antidote is not established to treat the Rot.
- The [[Storylines/Adventures/The Prodigal Son|Prodigal Son]] conflict still concerns his safety and duty to the Hospital District; the new reward does not replace that dilemma.

## Backstory

Taran inherited privilege but did not experience it as a reason to withdraw. His technical education made him unusually valuable in a city where skilled infrastructure workers are scarce. Returning to Old Ostium became first an act of conscience and then a practical commitment he cannot easily abandon.

## What They Know

- Local water distribution in the Hospital District.
- Working knowledge of [[Locations/Old Ostium Waterworks|Old Ostium Waterworks]].
- Enough about [[Locations/Old Ostium Pump 4|Old Ostium Pump 4]] and [[Locations/Old Ostium Pump 7|Old Ostium Pump 7]] to recognize when a problem is systemic.
- He does not know the deep truth of [[Locations/Titan Works|Titan Works]] or [[Locations/The Heart|The Heart]].

## Current Plans

- Keep the settlement functioning.
- Hide/minimize his symptoms until a solution exists.
- Resist being removed by force.
- Accept help if the party can separate 'leave now' from 'abandon everyone.'
