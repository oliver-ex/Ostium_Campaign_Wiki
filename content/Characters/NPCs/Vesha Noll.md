## At a Glance

**Role in campaign:** Shattered Axe intelligence chief and likely blackmail intermediary\
**Usual Location:** [[Locations/Old Ostium Slums|Old Ostium Slums]]\
**Current agenda:** Build a network of compromised people and routes that lets the gang reach into New Ostium.\
**Disposition toward party:** Curious about competent outsiders; would rather own leverage over the party than kill them immediately.\
**Voice / portrayal:** Soft-spoken, remembers details people wish she had forgotten.\
**What changes their mind:** Better leverage, protection from Ulroc, or proof that his rule is becoming a losing bet.\

## Secrets

- Vesha maintains blackmail files and informants.
- She is a natural contact for coercing the party into [[Storylines/Adventures/The Heist|The Heist]].
- Her loyalty is pragmatic rather than affectionate; she may abandon Ulroc if his power clearly collapses.

## Motives

- Information is safer than strength if nobody knows how much you have.
- Stay useful enough that Ulroc cannot discard her.

## Hidden Alliances & Relationships

- [[Characters/NPCs/Ulroc Grimscar|Ulroc Grimscar]] is employer, protector, and potential threat.
- Berrik Thane depends on her scouting for major raids.

## Backstory

Vesha survived the city's breakdown by becoming the person who knew who was desperate, indebted, corrupt, frightened, or secretly crossing the wall.

## What They Know

- Smuggling contacts.
- Blackmail targets.
- New Ostium museum and merchant security rumours.

## Current Plans

- Acquire a piece of leverage over the party.
- Arrange deniable thefts without exposing Shattered Axe leadership.
