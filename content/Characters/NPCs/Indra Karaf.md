## At a Glance

**Species (D&D 2024):** Goliath

**Role in campaign:** Morally complicated protector and faction leader\
**Usual Location:** [[Locations/Old Ostium Town Hall|Old Ostium Town Hall]]\
**Current agenda:** Keep the Protectorate fed, equipped, and strong enough to prevent raiders and beasts from overrunning protected settlements.\
**Disposition toward party:** Professional until the party threatens people under her protection or treats the costs of security as imaginary.\
**Voice / portrayal:** Low, economical, military; rarely wastes a sentence.\
**What changes their mind:** A plan that actually replaces the material resources or security her current levies provide.\

## Secrets

- Indra's high protection price is not a hidden extortion scheme; the costs really are severe.
- She knows some officers abuse the system at the margins and must decide how hard to police them without weakening the force.
- She may cooperate with New Ostium if it does not require surrendering Old Ostium residents to New Ostium priorities.

## Motives

- Justice in a place where justice has no functioning state behind it.
- Responsibility for the people who rely on her.
- Fear that moral purity without supplies will simply get everyone killed.

## Hidden Alliances & Relationships

- [[Characters/NPCs/Calev Marr|Calev Marr]] is her quartermaster and one of the people who can show the actual cost structure.
- The [[Factions/Old Ostium Hospital District Community|Old Ostium Hospital District — Community]] pays a painful but necessary levy.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Ulroc Grimscar|Ulroc Grimscar]] is a strategic enemy, not merely a personal rival.

## Backstory

Indra survived a major war and returned with a practical understanding of what organized violence costs. When most formal police and state authority withdrew, she helped pull together the people who were willing and able to defend neighbourhoods. The Protectorate became an institution because ad hoc heroism was not enough.

## What They Know

- Protectorate patrols and shortages.
- Old Ostium settlement politics.
- Shattered Axe raiding patterns.
- Enough infrastructure risk to respect the Engineers, but not the deep secret of the Titan Works.

## Current Plans

- Maintain levy collection.
- Prevent the Shattered Axe from becoming the uncontested armed power.
- Seek equipment and food from any source that does not compromise the Protectorate beyond recovery.

### Experiences

- **Commander:** +3
- **Battlefield Judgment:** +2

### Features

- **Hold the Line — Passive:** Allied defenders within Close range have advantage on reaction rolls made to resist being forced out of a defended position.
- **Veteran's Order — Action:** Mark a Stress to let an allied Protectorate adversary move and immediately make a standard attack when next spotlighted.
- **Interpose — Reaction:** When an ally within Very Close range would take Severe damage, Indra may mark a Stress to reduce the fictionally plausible harm by stepping into the threat; adjudicate the resulting damage on Indra.

### Fear Features

- **No One Breaks:** Spend a Fear when the Protectorate is about to lose cohesion; Indra restores order, clears one Stress from each nearby Protectorate ally, and immediately changes the battlefield posture.
