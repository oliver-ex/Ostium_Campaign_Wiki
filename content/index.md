## The Setting

Ostium is a divided city whose failing infrastructure, unequal access to care, and competing civic loyalties lead into ancient works and a wounded divine being. Old and New Ostium remain morally complicated, physically interconnected communities.

[[Locations/Ostium|Ostium]] lies in the Shoulderlands. [[Locations/Mistbreak|Mistbreak]] is an independent town far north with its own government and factions, and a possible destination in the search for a Frigid Woe antidote.
