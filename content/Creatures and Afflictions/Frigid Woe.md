# Frigid Woe

<!-- page-summary:start -->

An ancient Auren weapon affliction resistant to divine healing, driving a search for scarce antidote; it is distinct from the Rot.

<!-- page-summary:end -->


## History & Origin

- Ancient affliction originating as a weapon of [[World Lore/Auren Culture|Auren Culture]], formerly of [[Locations/Torney Isles|Torney Isles]].
- Designed for use against the gods' warriors; its effects resist divine healing.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Veritas|Veritas]] died of it in [[Locations/Mistbreak|Mistbreak]] several months before current events.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]]'s wife, [[Characters/NPCs/Tara Lusat|Tara Lusat]], died from the **Rot of Old Ostium**, not Frigid Woe. Her death explains his protectiveness, while northern Frigid Woe reports prompted the separate antidote purchase.

## Known Symptoms & Progression

- **Steven’s table — confirmed:** [[Characters/Player Characters/Kalim Escara|Kalim Escara]] became infected in [[Session Logs/Steven's Table/Session 3|Session 3]]. Exposure mechanism and symptoms are not recorded.
- **Oliver’s table — confirmed:** two pregen party members drank a vial in [[Session Logs/Oliver's Table/Session 1|Session 1]] and are now affected. They do not yet know the diagnosis or danger.
- **Oliver’s table — GM-established progression:** without antidote, those two characters will turn to blocks of ice and die in roughly a month. This is an approximate campaign prognosis, not a fixed official rules countdown; elapsed time and prior symptoms remain TBD. Do not apply it retroactively to Steven’s table without a ruling.
- The disease is transmitted by breathing in blue spores that Auren’s mages created long ago. When a creature comes into contact with these spores, it must succeed on a **DC 11 Constitution saving throw** or become infected with frigid woe. It takes **1d4 days** for the symptoms to manifest in an infected creature. These symptoms include fatigue, chills, and visible blue veins that appear on the creature’s body. The infected creature’s **speed is reduced by 5 feet** as long as it remains infected. **Every 10 days** after symptoms appear, an infected creature must succeed on a **DC 11 Constitution saving throw**, or its speed is **reduced by another 5 feet**. If a creature’s speed is **reduced to 0** as a result of this disease, the creature **dies and its body turns into a statue made of ice.**
- Distinct from the **Rot**; resemblance, shared origin, or a causal relationship is not established.

## Relationship to Divine Magic

- Designed to inflict suffering that divine healing could not cure. Do not generalize this to all divine magic or ordinary medical support.
- Exact interaction with PC features and existing campaign rulings: TBD. Do not introduce a new mechanical immunity.

## Recorded Retrieval — Steven’s Table

In [[Session Logs/Steven's Table/Session 5|Session 5]], the party killed Salsvault’s undead scientist and recovered **20 doses**: **17 sent to Mistbreak**, **one used** on an unnamed member infected in the laboratory, and **two retained**. The scientist had made himself and his workers undead to continue researching a disease intended to kill the gods. His name, the treated member’s identity, Kalim’s treatment, and Mistbreak receipt/distribution remain unconfirmed. This does not establish a cure or available stock for Oliver’s party.
