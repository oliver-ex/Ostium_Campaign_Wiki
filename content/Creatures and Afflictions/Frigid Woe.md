# Frigid Woe


## History & Origin

- Ancient affliction originating as a weapon of [[World Lore/Auren Culture|Auren Culture]], formerly of [[Locations/Torney Isles|Torney Isles]].
- Designed for use against the gods' warriors; its effects resist divine healing.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Veritas|Veritas]] died of it in [[Locations/Mistbreak|Mistbreak]] several months before current events.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]]'s wife, [[Characters/NPCs/Tara Lusat|Tara Lusat]], died from the **Rot of Old Ostium**, not Frigid Woe. Her death explains his protectiveness, while northern Frigid Woe reports prompted the separate antidote purchase.
