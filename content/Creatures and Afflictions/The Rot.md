## Established Facts

- Known as a danger in [[Locations/Old Ostium|Old Ostium]]; its symptoms and pressures affect cognition and higher mental function.
- The deeper explanation is infection of the wounded being at [[Locations/The Heart|The Heart]], associated with sewage/contamination entering its wound through failing infrastructure. See [[Storylines/Ostium Campaign|Ostium Campaign]] for the canonical campaign spine.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Taran Lusat|Taran Lusat]] is already afflicted at an early, possibly curable stage; his condition is not assumed known to the PCs.
- Distinct from [[Creatures and Afflictions/Frigid Woe|Frigid Woe]]. No Auren connection is established.

- [[Characters/NPCs/Tara Lusat|Tara Lusat]], wife of [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]] and mother of [[Characters/NPCs/Taran Lusat|Taran Lusat]] and [[Characters/NPCs/Sara Lusat|Sara Lusat]], died from the Rot of Old Ostium. Her date of death remains TBD.
