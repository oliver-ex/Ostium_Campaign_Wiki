# Session 0


## Session Goals

- Introduce the premise of [[Locations/Ostium|Ostium]] without revealing deep lore.
- Establish the visible contrast between [[Locations/New Ostium|New Ostium]] and [[Locations/Old Ostium|Old Ostium]].
- Confirm the existing PCs and their D&D 5e (2024) choices collaboratively; Daggerheart is an optional separate interpretation.
- Give each PC a reason they might accept work that crosses the wall.
- End with a clear path into [[Storylines/Adventures/The Prodigal Son|The Prodigal Son]] rather than forcing a single party origin.

## Player-Facing Premise

Ostium was once one great capital. Now the safer newer city stands behind walls while the old city sinks, floods, and survives beyond them. Armies cannot safely occupy Old Ostium, but small groups can still cross.

## Character-Creation Prompts

Ask each player:

- What ties you to New Ostium, Old Ostium, or both?
- Who do you know who could not simply leave the old city?
- What have you heard about the Rot that you believe?
- What kind of job would make you cross the wall?
- Which other PC have you already relied on before the campaign begins?

Use the table's chosen system for character creation. These questions establish narrative connections without assigning features or changing existing builds.

## Five Spotlight Anchors

Use a few, not necessarily all:

1. [[Locations/New Ostium|New Ostium]] — safety, walls, limited resources.
2. [[Locations/New Ostium Market District|New Ostium Market District]] — trade, eccentric merchants, wealth.
3. [[Locations/New Ostium Town Hall|New Ostium Town Hall]] — government and policy.
4. The distant/controlled boundary with [[Locations/Old Ostium|Old Ostium]].
5. Rumours of the [[Locations/Old Ostium Waterworks|Old Ostium Waterworks]] and the people who still maintain it.

## NPC Faces to Seed

- [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]] — wealthy trader with a family problem.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Trine Baradur|Trine Baradur]] — merchant who makes Old Ostium sound profitable.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Helena Orst|Helena Orst]] — shows government priorities if civic context is needed.
- A courier/guard/ordinary resident can be improvised to make the city feel lived in.

## Party Assembly

Do not force a single origin. Viable structures already discussed:

- job posting;
- inn/meeting place;
- hiring office;
- individual referrals to Egbert;
- PCs already know one another and simply need a first paid job.

The important outcome is that the party can plausibly accept Egbert's offer together.

## Closing Beat

If useful, end with a distant alarm, bells, or visible disturbance from Old Ostium to make the wall feel permeable and to lead into the first job.

## Do Not Reveal Yet

- Taran's Rot diagnosis.
- Titan Works.
- The Heart.
- The Rot's divine-infection origin.
- Rot King.

_No encounter or revelation recorded yet; this is a continuity note, not a publishing restriction._

## D&D 5e (2024) Resolution

Use [[Rules/System Guide|System Guide]] for approach-based ability checks. Proposed scenario DCs: 10 for a straightforward uncertain task and 15 for a demanding one. Set combat profiles, saving throws, and conditions only after verifying the actual opposition/effect and attending PCs. Existing Daggerheart tier, Fear, and Stress notes apply only when running that system. No disease progression or magic interaction is newly defined here.

## Daggerheart Resolution

Retain the authored scene guidance and Fear opportunities as campaign homebrew. For an uncertain action use the relevant trait and a separately chosen Difficulty; do not reuse a D&D DC by conversion. Success with Fear still achieves the stated goal; consequences should arise from the established situation. Keep essential clues accessible.
