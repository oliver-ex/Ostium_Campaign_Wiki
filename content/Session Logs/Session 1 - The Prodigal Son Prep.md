# Session 1 — The Prodigal Son


## Session Goals

- Make [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]] sympathetic rather than a simple controlling father.
- Cross into Old Ostium early enough that the campaign premise becomes concrete.
- Introduce [[Characters/NPCs/Taran Lusat|Taran Lusat]] doing visibly valuable work.
- Give players meaningful RP/exploration and an optional combat pressure rather than a mandatory fight.
- End with a decision or problem that points toward the waterworks.

## Opening

Egbert hires the group to bring Taran back from the [[Locations/Old Ostium Hospital District|Old Ostium Hospital District]].

## Optional Frigid Woe Reward Hook

[[Characters/NPCs/Shanda Karth|Shanda Karth]] may introduce the party to Egbert, whose wife, [[Characters/NPCs/Tara Lusat|Tara Lusat]], died of the Rot of Old Ostium. He owns the two vials she holds, one reserved for each son. He can negotiate transfer for resolving Taran's situation, a substantial purchase, collateral/assurances for immediate help, or a replacement plan from [[Locations/Mistbreak|Mistbreak]]. Use [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat#Antidote Negotiation — GM Quick Reference|his boundaries]]; keep the existing Taran/Hospital District objective and multiple resolutions.

Neither son is established as infected with Frigid Woe. Taran's Rot remains separate, and the antidote is not a Rot cure. Declining this job leaves other paid work, courier, northern travel, research, and theft available. Do not impose a new deadline.

## Likely Scenes

1. Egbert briefing in [[Locations/New Ostium|New Ostium]].
2. Controlled crossing / first Old Ostium exploration.
3. Hospital District arrival and meeting [[Characters/NPCs/Mara Voss|Mara Voss]].
4. Taran at a water/infrastructure problem.
5. Debate over leaving, treatment, and responsibility.
6. Optional interruption: Shattered Axe pressure, a Rot-afflicted threat, or a sudden water failure.

## Rot Clues

Use only subtle signs:
- lapse or fixation;
- momentary sensory/cognitive oddity;
- fatigue;
- another NPC watching Taran with concern.

Do not announce “Taran has the Rot” on first meeting unless player action clearly uncovers it.

## Optional Combat

A Shattered Axe raid or Rot threat can interrupt, but the purpose is:
- show Old Ostium danger;
- give combat-oriented PCs a spotlight;
- create a concrete infrastructure/community stake.

It should not solve Taran's moral dilemma by making one side obviously right.

## End / Next Hooks

Any resolution can lead toward:
- more work for the Hospital District;
- [[Factions/Old Ostium Engineers|Old Ostium Engineers]];
- a worsening water issue;
- eventually [[Storylines/Adventures/Pump 7|Pump 7]].
