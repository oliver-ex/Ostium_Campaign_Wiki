**Planned — not played.** Run [[Storylines/Adventures/The Road to Ostium|The Road to Ostium]] using its full situations, NPC agendas, clue matrix, and branches. This note is the table-specific outline, not a second adventure copy.

**Established starting facts:** in [[Session Logs/Oliver's Table/Session 1|Session 1]], the pregen party cleared the Wizard’s Tower Brewery basement and two members unknowingly drank Frigid Woe. They do not yet know the diagnosis, lethal prognosis, or need for an antidote. Untreated, they will turn to ice and die in roughly a month; the exact time remaining is TBD.

## Likely Scenes & Opposition

1. **Caravan opening:** establish the journey and invite character interaction. Describe only illness signs already established or agreed before play.
2. **Road ambush:** use the adventure’s warning signs and supply-theft stakes. Support detection, avoidance, negotiation, combat, retreat, or surrender. Carl’s predicament is optional; no death or letter discovery is assumed.
3. **Arrival:** contrast Old and New Ostium through daily life and access to help. Let the party choose contacts and routes.
4. **Discovering the danger:** if they consult Arla, she can identify or suspect Frigid Woe, distinguish it from the Rot, and point toward Shanda. If they bypass her, Shanda or another plausible medical contact can provide the lead. They learn the prognosis only when it is actually introduced.
5. **Antidote options:** Shanda holds Egbert’s two reserved vials and cannot independently sell them. Offer an introduction and discuss a northern supply/courier lead. Egbert can negotiate compensation for [[Storylines/Adventures/The Prodigal Son|The Prodigal Son]], a sale, an advance against assurances, or replacement arrangements; no quest is compulsory.
6. **End on a choice:** seek local treatment, negotiate, earn funds, arrange retrieval, travel north, investigate, or pursue another plan. Record the actual decision rather than forcing a route.
