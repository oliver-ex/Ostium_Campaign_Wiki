# Session 1 — The Wizard’s Tower Brewery

## Recap & Actual Events

A pregen party cleared the basement of the Wizard’s Tower Brewery. Two party members drank from a [[Creatures and Afflictions/Frigid Woe|Frigid Woe]] vial without knowing what it contained. Both are now affected by Frigid Woe.

## Clues Introduced & PC Knowledge

The two characters drank the contents, but do not yet know that they are affected, what the affliction does, or that they need an antidote. No diagnosis or warning has been recorded.

## Consequences — GM Knowledge

**GM-established campaign premise:** without the antidote, the two affected characters will die in roughly a month, turning into blocks of ice. This is an approximate narrative prognosis, not an exact session countdown or a newly asserted official disease mechanic. The elapsed time, affected characters’ identities, and any symptoms already shown are TBD.

## Where We Left Off / Next Preparation

The brewery basement is cleared. Prepare [[Storylines/Adventures/The Road to Ostium|The Road to Ostium]] for Session 2, allowing the party to learn the danger through play. Do not assume the pregen party has been replaced or identify them with any named PCs without confirmation.
