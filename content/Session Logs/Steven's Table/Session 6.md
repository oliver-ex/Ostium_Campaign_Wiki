# Session 6 — The Library Commission — Preparation

<!-- page-summary:start -->

The Buyer offers a library retrieval job leading toward Ostium; a disease manuscript and goblins burning books for warmth are tentative.

<!-- page-summary:end -->


## Session Goals & Opening Situation

**Canon starting point:** [[Session Logs/Steven's Table/Session 5|Session 5]] ended with 20 antidote doses recovered: 17 sent to Mistbreak, one used, two retained. **Planned:** [[Characters/NPCs/The Buyer|The Buyer]] learns of the find and offers a job recovering an artifact or information from an ancient ruined library for delivery to [[Locations/Ostium|Ostium]]. Acceptance has not occurred.

## Participants, Locations & Stakes

The party and The Buyer in the [[Locations/Torney Isles|Torney Isles]]. **Tentative:** the library stands within a cavern, now occupied by many goblins using its books as fuel against the cold. Its name, exact location, history, and occupants’ identities are TBD.

## Likely Scenes & Opposition

1. **Commission — Planned:** negotiate retrieval, payment, and delivery terms with The Buyer. His chest’s possible Ostium destination needs confirmation; personal delivery remains an option.
2. **Library — Tentative:** discover books being burned for warmth. Offer negotiation or replacement fuel, stealth, investigation, combat, or retreat; the goblins’ need for shelter gives them a reason to bargain.
3. **Recovery — Tentative:** seek a particular book of poisons and diseases that people in Ostium hope could illuminate [[Creatures and Afflictions/The Rot|the Rot]]. Contents and usefulness are undecided; it is not an established cure or evidence linking the Rot to Frigid Woe.

## NPC Agendas & Dialogue

- **The Buyer:** independent businessman protecting his trade position. **Suggested prompt:** “There is something in that library someone in Ostium wants. Let us agree what bringing it back is worth.”
- **Goblins — Tentative:** want warmth and a defensible home; books are fuel. Their response depends on what the party offers or threatens.
- **Ostium recipient — TBD:** a wealthy merchant, museum curator, or shady contact; a [[Factions/New Ostium Merchants Guild|Merchants Guild]] connection is possible, not established.

## Branches, Consequences & Pacing

- **Agreement / stealth / conflict:** any approach can lead to recovery; track damage and relations from actual choices.
- **Delay / retreat:** book-burning creates pressure, but do not destroy the essential lead offscreen merely to force haste; surviving fragments or testimony can preserve a route onward.
- **Decline:** the party keeps its two doses and can pursue other business. A negotiated antidote shipment via The Buyer is a separate possible route toward Ostium, not conditional on accepting the job.
