## Operational Summary

**Why the party comes here:** Act III destination and physical centre of the campaign's deepest wound\
**What is changing:** The interred god is bleeding and infected as Old Ostium's collapse contaminates the system around it.\
**Current controlling pressure:** Divine infection, unstable cognition, failing containment/stabilization, and the consequences of any intervention.\
**Hidden danger:** A personified infection such as the Rot King may emerge here or act through the Rot, but this remains a development choice rather than mandatory fixed canon.\

## Secrets & Hidden History

- The Heart belongs to the ancient/outer/old god captured and interred by the Titans.
- Its vitality was used as an accelerant that helped produce Ostium's extraordinary creativity, imagination, prosperity, and technological achievement. It is not the universal source of magic or reason.
- Damage to the waterworks allows fluid loss: the being is slowly bleeding.
- Raw sewage and contamination from the collapsing city have infected its wounds.
- The Rot is connected to that infection and therefore expresses itself strongly through cognition and higher mental function.
- The being's death would not reduce everyone to mindless shells, but the extraordinary boon associated with its vitality could be lost.

## Faction Pressures

- Every surface faction will have different interests once the truth becomes known: cure, exploit, conceal, abandon, or weaponize.
- No faction should automatically know the whole truth before the party earns/reveals it.

## Hidden Areas / Access

- Chambers around the Heart can contain Titan stabilizers, contaminated inflow, damaged outflow, and routes that explain the Pump/Waterworks network.

## Clues & Revelations

- The Rot's 'madness' becomes legible as infection patterns, pain, intrusive connection, and distorted cognition.
- Evidence should support an endgame option to cure the infection rather than merely kill a monster.

### Features

- **Pulse Through the City — Passive:** Major changes here should have visible consequences in distant waterworks and potentially in the emotional/cognitive atmosphere above.
