## Operational Summary

**Why the party comes here:** Hub for political quests and formal Old/New negotiation\
**What is changing:** Pump 7 and refugee pressure can force the government to engage with Old Ostium.\
**Current controlling pressure:** Capacity limits, legitimacy, public opinion.\
**Hidden danger:** Some officials privately accept more interdependence than public policy admits.\
**Useful NPCs:** [[Characters/NPCs/Helena Orst|Helena Orst]]\

## Secrets & Hidden History

- Useful records can reveal exactly how dependent New Ostium remains on old infrastructure.
- This is the natural site for later [[Storylines/Adventures/Negotiations|Negotiations]].

## Faction Pressures

- Merchants lobby heavily.
- Security officials want hard boundaries.
- Civic planners understand that water systems ignore those boundaries.

## Hidden Areas / Access

- Records rooms, planning offices, and controlled council chambers.

## Clues & Revelations

- Contradictions between public claims of separation and infrastructure maps.
