## Operational Summary

**Why the party comes here:** Megadungeon threshold: mundane civic system gradually revealing older infrastructure\
**What is changing:** Municipal failures are exposing deeper structures and contaminating them.\
**Current controlling pressure:** Maintenance scarcity, water pressure, Rot, political dependency.\
**Hidden danger:** The deepest 'waterworks' are part of the interred being's circulatory system or interfaces built around it.\

## Secrets & Hidden History

- Ostium did not create the deepest system. Municipal engineers tapped into and built over existing structures.
- The Titan-built layers direct the being's energies and stabilize wounds inflicted during capture.
- Fluid loss from the failing system is effectively the interred god bleeding.

## Faction Pressures

- Engineers know strange legacy infrastructure exists but are not cultists and do not know the whole truth.
- Both cities need the system enough that destruction is rarely a clean solution.

## Hidden Areas / Access

- Lower galleries lead into [[Locations/Titan Works|Titan Works]].
- Some conduits are not water pipes at all, even if surface engineers interpret them that way.

## Clues & Revelations

- Changes in pressure resemble pulse.
- Old repairs refer to 'walls' and 'channels' that are actually interfaces with living tissue or divine material.
- Contamination travels in ways municipal hydrology alone cannot explain.

### Features

- **Layer Beneath Layer — Passive:** Whenever the party believes they have reached the oldest construction, leave evidence of something older underneath.
- **Flow Finds a Way — Action:** Water, waste, or other fluid takes a route that changes access, reveals a chamber, or threatens a populated district.
- **Living Analogy — Reaction:** On Fear during deep investigation, provide useful structural information through an unsettling anatomical resemblance.
