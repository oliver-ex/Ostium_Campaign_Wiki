## Role in the Campaign

Bring the campaign's civic, psychological, and mythic layers together at the divine wound, then return the consequences to the people and institutions above.

## Premise

The party reaches [[Locations/The Heart|The Heart]], learns how Old Ostium's collapse has contaminated and drained the interred god, and confronts the infection directly. The endgame is about treatment, exploitation, loss, and what relationship Ostium will have to the power beneath it.

## Major Story Beats

1. Descend through the deepest Titan Works toward the Heart.
2. Reveal that the captured being's vitality acted as an accelerant for Ostium's extraordinary creativity, imagination, prosperity, and technological achievement without being the universal source of magic or reason.
3. Make the divine infection legible: sewage/contamination and structural failure have turned an ancient wound into the Rot crisis.
4. If developed, bring the Rot King into focus as an agent/personification of infection with powerful cognitive effects.
5. Use faction consequences from Acts I-II to determine who can assist, obstruct, exploit, or negotiate around the final intervention.
6. Preserve the established possibility of curing the infection.
7. Resolve the Heart's condition and the status of the Titan systems through meaningful player choice rather than a single mandatory kill.
8. Show surface consequences in Old Ostium, New Ostium, the waterworks, and the city's future capacity to rebuild.

## Revelations to Seed

- The being's boon explains Ostium's historical exceptionalism without making all reason/magic dependent on it.
- The being can die without everyone becoming mindless, but the city may lose a supernatural advantage.
- Cure is a real option.

## Act End State

The state of the Heart, the Rot, and Ostium's political relationship is changed decisively. The city must then rebuild—or fail to—under the consequences the party chose.

## Open Questions / Keep Flexible

- Exact final choices beyond the already discussed cure/death consequences remain open.
- Do not finalize the Rot King's metaphysics until campaign development makes one version useful.
