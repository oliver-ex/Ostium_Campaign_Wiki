## Role in the Campaign

Shift the campaign from surface civic breakdown into the ancient infrastructure beneath it while allowing faction conflicts and side adventures to continue changing access and resources.

## Premise

The party follows clues from Pump 7 into older layers, learning that the municipal waterworks were built into a pre-existing system. The Rot's strange cognitive effects become more legible through places such as the University.

## Major Story Beats

1. Explore deeper sections of [[Locations/Old Ostium Waterworks|Old Ostium Waterworks]] and first sustained areas of [[Locations/Titan Works|Titan Works]].
2. Use [[Storylines/Adventures/Rescue Mission|Rescue Mission]] and [[Locations/Old Ostium University|Old Ostium University]] to show that Rot-afflicted cognition can synchronize, distort, and occasionally reveal fragments of truth.
3. Escalate Shattered Axe pressure through [[Storylines/Adventures/The Heist|The Heist]], [[Storylines/Adventures/The Best Defense|The Best Defense]], or other faction moves depending on player relationships.
4. Use [[Storylines/Adventures/Heist II|Heist II]] or merchant negotiations to make material support for Old Ostium a player-driven problem.
5. Reveal that Titan structures were built around something wounded and alive, serving as bindings, conduits, and stabilization.
6. Make it clear that damage and contamination in the civic system are affecting the ancient system rather than merely sitting above it.
7. Move toward the recognition that the Rot is an infection associated with the wounded being.

## Revelations to Seed

- The Titan Works predate Ostium.
- The structures are both binding/extraction and wound-stabilization.
- The Rot has a relationship to the wounded being's cognitive influence.
- The system is suffering contamination and fluid loss.

## Act End State

The party understands that a living divine being is interred beneath Ostium and that the city's Rot/infrastructure crisis is threatening it, setting up descent to the Heart.

## Open Questions / Keep Flexible
