## Role in the Campaign

Establish the campaign's social geography, demonstrate that Old Ostium is a living community rather than a disposable ruin, and make shared infrastructure the bridge into the deeper plot.

## Premise

Start from a comprehensible human problem—[[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]] wants his son back—and widen the frame until the party sees that individual rescue, faction politics, and city-scale water systems cannot be separated.

## Major Story Beats

1. Introduce [[Locations/New Ostium|New Ostium]] as orderly, civilized, and constrained rather than villainous.
2. Run [[Storylines/Adventures/The Prodigal Son|The Prodigal Son]]: Egbert hires the party to retrieve Taran; the party discovers that Taran is genuinely helping the Hospital District and cannot simply leave without consequences.
3. Show early Rot signs in Taran without announcing the diagnosis immediately.
4. Introduce Old Ostium's factions through practical needs: the Hospital settlement, Engineers, Protectorate, and Shattered Axe pressure.
5. Allow side jobs to build relationships and resources before the act climax.
6. Escalate shared-water and waste problems into [[Storylines/Adventures/Pump 7|Pump 7]].
7. Present a bad binary that benefits one side of Ostium at serious cost to the other.
8. Have [[Characters/NPCs/Brock Drungur|Brock Drungur]] reveal the dangerous third option: repair the exhaust system.
9. Resolve Pump 7 through player action and reveal the first compelling evidence of a deeper pre-Ostium system.
