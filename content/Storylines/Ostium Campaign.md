## Purpose in the Campaign

- Provide New Ostium as a functioning hub and Old Ostium as an inhabited urban megadungeon/open exploration space.
- Move from human-scale family and faction problems into infrastructure, ancient systems, and the divine wound below.
- Keep moral choices relational: who bears costs, who is protected, and what kind of city can be rebuilt.

## Core Truth

The deepest waterworks interface with an ancient god captured and wounded by the Titans. Its vitality helped accelerate Ostium's historical creativity and prosperity. The collapse of Old Ostium is causing fluid loss and contamination; sewage entering the wound has contributed to a divine infection expressed as the Rot. The campaign descends from civic symptoms to the wounded source.

## Major Beats

1. Act I establishes the twin cities through [[Storylines/Adventures/The Prodigal Son|The Prodigal Son]], living Old Ostium communities, and competing factions.
2. Act I culminates in [[Storylines/Adventures/Pump 7|Pump 7]], proving that political separation cannot sever shared infrastructure and opening a route/clue toward deeper works.
3. Act II explores older layers, faction escalation, the cognitive weirdness of [[Locations/Old Ostium University|Old Ostium University]], and evidence of [[Locations/Titan Works|Titan Works]].
4. Act III descends to [[Locations/The Heart|The Heart]], confronts the infection and its possible personification as the Rot King, and resolves what happens to the wounded being and Ostium above.

## Factions in Motion

- Engineers preserve the system.
- Protectorate/Shattered Axe conflict gives the old city human stakes.
- Government/Guild conflict and cooperation define the new city.
- Hospital District gives consequences a specific human face.

## NPC Agendas

- Taran connects family, settlement, Rot, and waterworks.
- Brock connects municipal engineering to the deeper layer.
- Indra and Helena represent competing but defensible institutional responsibilities.
- Ulroc represents predation inside collapse.

## Branches & Player Choices

- The party may side differently with factions without leaving the main arc.
- Side quests can change resources, access, and political willingness before major story beats.
- The Rot King remains a possible major antagonist, not a required answer to every Rot phenomenon.

## Consequences

- The ending should alter the surface city, not remain an isolated dungeon victory.
- Death of the being is consequential but not apocalyptic mindlessness; it may remove the supernatural boon that helped Ostium flourish.
- A cure can preserve the possibility of a different relationship to the Heart.
