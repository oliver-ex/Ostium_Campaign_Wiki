# Quests and Prepared Adventures

The current premise is the PCs' search for a Frigid Woe antidote: [[Session Logs/Session Prep — The Road to Ostium|Session Prep — The Road to Ostium]]. Shanda can introduce [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]], who owns two reserved vials and may offer them as negotiated compensation for [[Storylines/Adventures/The Prodigal Son|The Prodigal Son]]. Purchase funded by other work, immediate help against assurances, replacement arrangements, courier retrieval, direct Mistbreak travel, research, or theft remain alternatives; accepting the quest is optional.
