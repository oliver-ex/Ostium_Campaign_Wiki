# Quests and Prepared Adventures

Shared adventure preparation includes the search for a Frigid Woe antidote: [[Storylines/Adventures/The Road to Ostium|The Road to Ostium]]. Shanda can introduce [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]], who owns two reserved vials and may offer them as negotiated compensation for [[Storylines/Adventures/The Prodigal Son|The Prodigal Son]]. Purchase funded by other work, immediate help against assurances, replacement arrangements, courier retrieval, direct Mistbreak travel, research, or theft remain alternatives; accepting the quest is optional. Table-specific outcomes belong in [[Session Logs/Session Logs|Session Logs]]: Steven’s table retrieved an antidote in Session 5; Oliver’s Road to Ostium is planned for Session 2. Prepared hooks below are not evidence of completed events.
