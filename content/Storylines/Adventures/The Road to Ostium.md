# The Road to Ostium

<!-- page-summary:start -->

A prepared caravan journey and city investigation opening several routes to Frigid Woe antidote through danger, medical referrals, and negotiation.

<!-- page-summary:end -->


**Prepared, not played.** The premise is established; staging and possible outcomes are **Tentative**. Use these beats in whatever order follows the party’s choices. Improvise dialogue, scenery, complications, and terms.

## Opening Situation

The party travels toward [[Locations/Ostium|Ostium]] with [[Characters/NPCs/Darian Auir|Darian Auir]]’s caravan, carrying food, medical supplies, and gold. The journey leads into a search for help with [[Creatures and Afflictions/Frigid Woe|Frigid Woe]].

**Table continuity:** at [[Session Logs/Oliver's Table/Session 1|Oliver’s table]], two pregen characters are affected but do not yet know the diagnosis or danger; let them discover it through play. [[Session Logs/Oliver's Table/Session 2|Session 2]] is planned. Steven’s table has already retrieved an antidote in Session 5; this is not automatically its next adventure.

## Adventure Beats

### 1. Caravan Journey and Ambush

[[Characters/NPCs/Korok Grund|Korok Grund]] leads [[Factions/The Shattered Axe|Shattered Axe]] bandits posing as New Ostium guards. They demand an inspection and claim the cargo is “contraband.” This is a robbery, with no established official involvement or genuine contraband.

- Mismatched clothing and weapons betray the disguise. If the party misses this, Darian warns them before supplies are surrendered.
- Korok wants the cargo. Fighting, bargaining, retreating, and bypassing the roadblock are all possible.
- Use [[Encounters/Roadside Ambush|Roadside Ambush]] for the map, roster, combat, and morale; it is prepared for three level-1 PCs. Reassess for actual attendance and resources. If using XP, its proposed award is 200 XP, including a peaceful resolution.

### 2. The Human Cost

[[Characters/NPCs/Carl Marsh|Carl Marsh]] needs food for his wife and medicine for his daughter in Old Ostium; her illness is unspecified. Conversation, surrender, or [[Attachments/Carl Marsh - Unsent Letter|his unsent letter]] can reveal this without killing him.

Let the party decide what to do with him. His desperation sits alongside the harm of stealing supplies meant for others; it does not excuse the faction’s cruelty.

### 3. Arrival in Ostium

- **[[Locations/Old Ostium|Old Ostium]]:** damaged streets, strained care, poverty, and residents keeping essential services working. The Rot is a major concern.
- **[[Locations/New Ostium|New Ostium]]:** greater stability and wealth, with fear of illness and unequal access to care.

Let the party choose where to seek help. Local suspicion or shortages can complicate that choice without fixing the route.

### 4. The Old Hospital Quarter

At [[Locations/Old Ostium Hospital District|the Hospital District]], [[Characters/NPCs/Arla Gerrin|Arla Gerrin]] assesses the established symptoms. She recognizes that this is **not the Rot**, identifies or strongly suspects Frigid Woe, and refers them to Shanda. She has no antidote.

The district’s shortages and competing needs provide room for interaction. Help is welcome; the referral is freely available.

### 5. Shanda Karth’s Apothecary

[[Characters/NPCs/Shanda Karth|Shanda Karth]] holds **two antidote vials owned by [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]]**, one reserved for each of his two sons. She cannot sell them without his authorization. She also knows a supply recently arrived in [[Locations/Mistbreak|Mistbreak]], an independent town far north of Ostium with its own government and factions. Reaching that supply requires a separate journey or a paid courier; travel time and access terms remain TBD.

**Optional: meet Egbert.** His wife [[Characters/NPCs/Tara Lusat|Tara]] died from the Rot; he bought the vials as a precaution after northern reports. Neither son is established as having Frigid Woe. He may negotiate a sale, advance, replacement arrangement, or medicine as compensation for [[Storylines/Adventures/The Prodigal Son|helping Taran]]. Preserve Taran’s duty to the Hospital District; helping him need not mean forcing him home.

### 6. Choosing Their Next Step

The party can negotiate for the local vials, earn money through other work, hire a courier, travel to Mistbreak, investigate further, or attempt theft. The Prodigal Son is an optional hook. End with their chosen direction and prepare from there.

## GM Essentials

- Keep the core leads accessible: this illness differs from the Rot, an antidote exists, and Mistbreak offers a route forward. If a scene is skipped, another plausible source can provide the lead; no essential revelation depends on one check.
- **Decide as needed:** symptoms and prior rulings, urgency, dose requirements, prices, travel/courier timing, and negotiated terms. Two vials do not establish enough treatment for everyone.
- Ancient origins remain a later research lead; local healers are not automatically experts. Keep the Rot and Frigid Woe distinct and divine mysteries unresolved.
- Record cargo losses, NPC fates, agreements, and what the PCs actually learn in the session report. Update [[Planning/Plot Threads and Mysteries|plot threads]] and the timeline from actual outcomes.
