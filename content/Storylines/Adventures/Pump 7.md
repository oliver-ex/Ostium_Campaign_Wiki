## Function in the Larger Arc

Act I climax. Converts the campaign's Old/New political divide into an unavoidable shared-infrastructure crisis and opens the deeper layer.

## Hook / Pressure

Water/overflow conditions tied to [[Locations/Old Ostium Pump 7|Old Ostium Pump 7]] reach a point where both cities need intervention. Prior expeditions have failed or been driven off by Rot conditions.

## Objective

Stabilize Pump 7 without accepting a solution that simply transfers catastrophe from one population to the other.

## Stakes

- Drinking water and waste/overflow control.
- Old Ostium settlement survival.
- New Ostium civic stability.
- The party's standing with Engineers and governments.

## Likely Scenes

These are modular possibilities, not a fixed sequence.

1. Stakeholder briefing(s) establish incompatible priorities.
2. Reach Pump 7 through changing waterworks conditions.
3. Diagnose the visible failure and discover the apparent bad binary.
4. Consult/encounter Brock, who identifies the dangerous exhaust repair as a third route.
5. Enter the exhaust/deeper service system and execute the repair under Rot/infrastructure pressure.
6. Resolve immediate flows and reveal evidence/access pointing toward pre-Ostium works.

## Obstacles & Complications

- Rot exposure.
- Time pressure.
- Mechanical failures that move rather than merely repeat.
- Political pressure to accept the 'easy' solution.
