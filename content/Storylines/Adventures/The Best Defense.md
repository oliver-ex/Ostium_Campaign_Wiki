## Function in the Larger Arc

Action-oriented side quest that gives the party a direct way to reduce Shattered Axe pressure and improve Old Ostium security.

## Hook / Pressure

A Shattered Axe raiding party is preparing another attack. Strike it before it can hit a settlement.

## Objective

Disrupt, scatter, capture, or destroy the raiding party.

## Stakes

- Supplies and lives in the target settlement.
- Shattered Axe momentum.
- Party standing with the Protectorate/Hospital District.

## Key NPCs

- [[Characters/NPCs/Indra Karaf|Indra Karaf]] or [[Characters/NPCs/Mara Voss|Mara Voss]] can provide the hook.
- Berrik Thane can be the raiding captain if a named opponent is useful.

## Likely Scenes

These are modular possibilities, not a fixed sequence.

1. Gather route/intelligence.
2. Choose ambush, sabotage, warning/evacuation, or negotiation with coerced raiders.
3. Hit the raiding force or its supplies.
4. Decide what to do with prisoners, stolen goods, and compromised locals.

## Obstacles & Complications

- Some raiders may be desperate recruits rather than ideologues.
- Hitting the force may expose who informed on the target.

## Consequences

### If successful

- Target settlement gains time and party reputation.

- Raid prevented but Shattered Axe learns more about party tactics.
