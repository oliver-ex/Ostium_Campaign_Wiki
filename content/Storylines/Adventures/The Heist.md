## Function in the Larger Arc

Shattered Axe coercion job that tests whether the party will commit a serious crime in civilized New Ostium and demonstrates non-combat consequences.

## Hook / Pressure

[[Characters/NPCs/Vesha Noll|Vesha Noll]] or another Shattered Axe intermediary coerces the party—potentially through blackmail—into stealing a valuable artifact from a New Ostium museum.

## Objective

Acquire the designated artifact without exposing the Shattered Axe's role.

## Stakes

- Criminal liability in New Ostium.
- Party reputation.
- Shattered Axe leverage.
- Whatever historical clue or market value the artifact carries.

## Key NPCs

- [[Characters/NPCs/Vesha Noll|Vesha Noll]] — likely handler.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Ulroc Grimscar|Ulroc Grimscar]] — ultimate beneficiary.
- A museum curator can be created when the adventure is activated.

## Likely Scenes

These are modular possibilities, not a fixed sequence.

1. Coercive meeting where the Blade proves it has leverage.
2. Legwork in civilized New Ostium: schedules, staff, legal access, donors, security.
3. Choose infiltration, social manipulation, forgery, bribery, or direct theft.
4. Complication creates witnesses/evidence rather than default combat.
5. Delivery choice: hand it over, betray the Blade, duplicate/substitute it, or expose the coercion.

## Obstacles & Complications

- Surveillance and records.
- Civilian staff.
- Legal consequences.
- The Blade may have lied about the artifact's importance.

### If successful

- Shattered Axe leverage may decrease or increase depending on delivery.
