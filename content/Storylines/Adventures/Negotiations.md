# Negotiations

<!-- page-summary:start -->

A prepared political adventure turning the cities’ unavoidable interdependence into workable cooperation, if their competing needs can be reconciled.

<!-- page-summary:end -->


## Function in the Larger Arc

Political adventure that attempts to convert unavoidable Old/New interdependence into explicit cooperation.

## Hook / Pressure

After enough shared crises, representatives need help negotiating a durable arrangement between Old Ostium communities and New Ostium institutions.

## Objective

Produce a workable agreement or limited cooperation framework.

## Stakes

- Water and infrastructure access.
- Refugee policy.
- Security.
- Recognition and resources.
- Political legitimacy on both sides.

## Likely Scenes

These are modular possibilities, not a fixed sequence.

1. Pre-negotiation meetings identify red lines and actual resource constraints.
2. Party decides who must be at the table and what leverage/evidence to bring.
3. Formal negotiation exposes incompatible definitions of responsibility.
4. A crisis, revelation, or faction move tests whether the proposed framework is real.
5. Agreement, limited accord, or breakdown.

## Obstacles & Complications

- Everyone has defensible constraints.
- Shattered Axe violence or public backlash can destabilize talks.
- Resources must be quantified enough that promises feel real.

## D&D 5e (2024) Scene Guidance

- **Possible checks:** Wisdom (Insight) to assess priorities; Charisma (Persuasion) to advocate a viable bargain. Use only for meaningful uncertain actions.
- **Suggested scenario DCs — Proposed homebrew:** 10 for a straightforward attempt under pressure; 15 for a demanding attempt. Adjust to the specific task; these are not converted Daggerheart numbers.
- **Stakes:** A check changes willingness within plausible limits, not another person's convictions automatically.
- **Opposition and balance:** choose verified 2024-compatible stat blocks, number of participants, terrain, and resource pressure for the actual PC levels and attendance. No unverified official block or CR assigned.
- **Saves / conditions / magic:** define only for an actual hazard or verified effect; existing spell features and table disease rulings need review before adjudication. See [[Rules/System Guide|System Guide]].

- **New Ostium Town Hall — Social at party tier.**
- **Crisis interruption — Event if needed.**

### Adversaries

- **Primarily Social opposition; combat should be an external complication, not the negotiation mechanic.**

### Fear Opportunities

- Fear creates political commitments, offended constituencies, leaked statements, or a crisis that forces a concession.

## Clues & Revelations

- Old/New dependence is already factual; the negotiation is about who acknowledges and pays for it.

## Meaningful Choices

- Broad peace, narrow infrastructure agreement, refugee quota, joint patrol/maintenance guarantees, or failure.

## Resolutions

- A limited practical agreement can be more credible than total reconciliation.

## Consequences

### If successful

- Cooperation improves final-act capacity and legitimacy.

### If partially successful

- One domain improves while another remains unresolved.

### If failed / abandoned

- Breakdown hardens faction positions but does not prevent the deep plot.

## Arc Connections

- Best after Pump 7 and other shared crises have produced concrete evidence of interdependence.
