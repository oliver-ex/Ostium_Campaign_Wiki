# Assassination

> No public adventure page exists yet. Create one from `_Templates/Adventure - Public.md` when the party learns about the job.

## Function in the Larger Arc

Later side quest that lets the party directly address Ulroc after the Shattered Axe's actions have crossed a threshold.

## Hook / Pressure

A patron from Old or New Ostium decides [[Characters/NPCs/Ulroc Grimscar|Ulroc Grimscar]] has gone too far and asks the party to kill him.

## Objective

Kill or permanently remove Ulroc as leader of the Shattered Axe.

## Stakes

- Retaliation.
- Power vacuum.
- Fate of coerced Shattered Axe members.
- Protectorate and settlement security.

- [[Characters/NPCs/Indra Karaf|Indra Karaf]] as possible patron/beneficiary, depending on play.

## Likely Scenes

These are modular possibilities, not a fixed sequence.

1. Determine Ulroc's patterns and defenses.
2. Choose direct assault, infiltration, betrayal, trap, or political removal.
3. Deal with Vesha and other lieutenants whose loyalties are not identical.
4. Confront Ulroc.
5. Handle the immediate succession/power-vacuum consequence.

## Obstacles & Complications

- Ulroc should not be alone just because the party found him.
- Killing him does not automatically erase the Shattered Axe.

## Clues & Revelations

- Ulroc's organization is personal enough that his death creates fragmentation, not clean institutional succession.

## Meaningful Choices

- Kill him, capture him, expose him, turn his lieutenants, or destroy his profitability/authority enough that he ceases to lead.

## Resolutions

- Any route that truly removes Ulroc's control satisfies the strategic objective even if he lives.

## Consequences

### If successful

- Shattered Axe fragments; settlements gain breathing room; another leader may emerge.

### If partially successful

- Ulroc falls but a lieutenant consolidates part of the network.

### If failed / abandoned

- Ulroc retaliates and becomes a more direct campaign enemy.

## Arc Connections

- Best in Act II/III after the party has enough history with the Blade for his death to matter.
