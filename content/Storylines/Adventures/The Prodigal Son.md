# The Prodigal Son

<!-- page-summary:start -->

A prepared family rescue that becomes a civic dilemma: Egbert wants Taran home, but the Hospital District depends on his engineering.

<!-- page-summary:end -->


## Function in the Larger Arc

Opening adventure that introduces both cities, makes Old Ostium morally concrete, and links the party to the waterworks through Taran.

## Hook / Pressure

[[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]] hires the party because he cannot safely retrieve Taran himself and believes his son is wasting his life in Old Ostium. His wife, [[Characters/NPCs/Tara Lusat|Tara Lusat]], died from [[Creatures and Afflictions/The Rot|the Rot of Old Ostium]], making him protective of his children, including [[Characters/NPCs/Sara Lusat|Sara Lusat]]. Taran's own Rot danger intensifies his fear of another family loss; the Frigid Woe antidote is not a Rot cure.

### Optional Antidote Hook

[[Characters/NPCs/Shanda Karth|Shanda Karth]] can introduce the PCs to Egbert while they seek treatment for Frigid Woe. She holds his **two reserved vials**, one per son. Egbert may offer them as compensation for assistance with the existing Taran situation; neither a second quest nor a newly missing son is introduced.

He is courteous and sympathetic: his wife's death makes their need real to him. Parting with the medicine also exposes his family to a risk he takes seriously. See [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat#Antidote Negotiation — GM Quick Reference|his negotiation priorities]].

## Objective

Reach Taran and resolve the conflict between Egbert's demand for safety and Taran's duty to the Hospital District.

## Stakes

- Taran's safety.
- The Hospital District's drinking water and infrastructure.
- Egbert's family trauma.
- The party's first reputation in both cities.

## Key NPCs

- [[Characters/NPCs/Egbert Lusat|Egbert Lusat]] — patron.

- [[Characters/NPCs/Mara Voss|Mara Voss]] — demonstrates settlement stakes.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Brock Drungur|Brock Drungur]] — optional engineering contact who needs capable help without losing essential workers.

## Likely Scenes

These are modular possibilities, not a fixed sequence. This preparation is unassigned and unplayed; it establishes no completed session. Make Egbert sympathetic, cross into Old Ostium early, and give room for conversation and exploration.

1. Egbert's briefing in New Ostium: establish love, fear, wealth, and his simplified view of Taran's situation.
2. Crossing into Old Ostium: show why small parties can move where armies cannot.
3. Arrival at [[Locations/Old Ostium Hospital District|the Hospital District]]: meet Mara and show Taran actively solving a problem or maintaining water rather than passively waiting.
4. Conversation with Taran and residents: reveal that leaving creates immediate practical risk.
5. Optional pressure scene: raiders, a Rot-afflicted threat, or a water failure gives players a combat/exploration route without requiring violence. Combat can spotlight danger and community stakes, but does not settle the family dilemma.
6. Resolution: force no single answer; let the party construct or fail to construct a compromise.

## Obstacles & Complications

- Taran refuses simple extraction.
- Egbert may have hired the party with the assumption that persuasion or force is acceptable.
- Taran's early Rot symptoms complicate any 'just let him stay' answer.
- The district lacks replacement expertise.

## Negotiated Antidote Compensation & Alternatives

| PC approach | Available agreement / outcome |
|---|---|
| Undertake this quest | Egbert may promise the two vials on successful completion under agreed terms. Define success explicitly against the existing Taran objective; a credible safety/settlement compromise can qualify if negotiated. |
| Offer gold | He may sell one or both for substantial payment sufficient to address replacement expense and risk. Exact price TBD. |
| Undertake other work | The PCs can earn money elsewhere in Ostium, then return to negotiate purchase; accepting this quest is not required. |
| Offer assurances, collateral, or immediate assistance | He may authorize transfer before completion if family safety and trust are adequately addressed; no compulsory wait for the entire adventure. |
| Propose replacement from Mistbreak | Negotiate courier or personal retrieval, payment, responsibility, and delivery risk. No guaranteed stock or arbitrary delivery deadline. |
| Bypass Egbert | Courier arrangements through Shanda, direct Mistbreak travel, investigation of other possible treatments, or theft remain real choices. Research does not establish an undiscovered cure automatically. |

Only two vials exist in this local stock. Negotiation changes ownership/access, not quantity, dose requirements, or established disease rules. Shanda releases them only on Egbert's authorization or an actual outcome in play. If the PCs decline the quest or fail it, northern leads remain available; honor any payment/advance terms actually agreed rather than inventing punitive conditions later.

## D&D 5e (2024) Scene Guidance

- **Possible checks:** Wisdom (Insight) to understand competing worries; Intelligence (Investigation) to assess evidence of water dependency. Use only for meaningful uncertain actions.
- **Suggested scenario DCs — Proposed homebrew:** 10 for a straightforward attempt under pressure; 15 for a demanding attempt. Adjust to the specific task; these are not converted Daggerheart numbers.
- **Stakes:** A voluntary agreement or a practical replacement plan matters more than a persuasion result.
- **Opposition and balance:** choose verified 2024-compatible stat blocks, number of participants, terrain, and resource pressure for the actual PC levels and attendance. No unverified official block or CR assigned.
- **Saves / conditions / magic:** define only for an actual hazard or verified effect; existing spell features and table disease rulings need review before adjudication. See [[Rules/System Guide|System Guide]].

## Daggerheart Scene Kit

> Preserved campaign homebrew; use its own tiers and rolls, independently from D&D.


### Environments

- **Hospital District — Tier 1 Social: shelter, ration, endure.**
- **Old Ostium Approach — Tier 1 Exploration: isolate, flood, expose.**

### Adversaries

- **Shattered Axe raider — Tier 1 Standard/Skulk if combat is desired.**
- **Rot-afflicted local threat — improvise to fit tone; do not make every afflicted person a monster.**

### Fear Opportunities

- A Fear result during travel can create Rot pressure, a blocked route, or a social cost.
- A Fear result during the Taran debate can make a concrete system fail, demonstrating rather than narrating the stakes.

## Clues & Revelations

- Taran's affliction should be noticed gradually: a lapse or fixation, a momentary sensory or cognitive oddity, fatigue, or another NPC’s concern. Reveal the diagnosis when player actions uncover it.
- Taran's local water expertise is genuinely essential.
- Egbert's fear is grounded in what happened to his wife.

## Meaningful Choices

- Bring Taran back immediately.
- Leave Taran in place.
- Seek treatment while arranging replacement technical capacity.
- Negotiate a temporary stay or rotating solution.
- Discover an entirely different solution through play.

## Resolutions

- The strongest resolution is likely a compromise that acknowledges both Taran's duty and Egbert's fear, but do not predetermine it.

## Consequences

### If successful

- Party gains Egbert's gratitude, Hospital District trust, or both depending on method.
- Waterworks become a natural next thread.

### If partially successful

- One side may feel betrayed while still remaining available for later repair of the relationship.

### If failed / abandoned

- Taran's condition or the district's water reliability can worsen, creating future urgency rather than campaign failure.

## Arc Connections

- Introduces the moral logic of Ostium.
- Seeds [[Storylines/Adventures/Pump 7|Pump 7]] through Taran/Brock and fragile water systems; further Hospital District work or contact with the Engineers can follow any resolution.
- Establishes the Rot as personal before it becomes cosmic.
