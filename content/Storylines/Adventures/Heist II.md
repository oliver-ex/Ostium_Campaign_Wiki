## Function in the Larger Arc

Morally grey resource quest: Old Ostium needs supplies/upgrades and the party can steal from concentrated New Ostium merchant wealth.

## Hook / Pressure

A settlement, Engineer crew, or Protectorate contact needs equipment that cannot be afforded or acquired normally. The party is asked to steal it from the [[Factions/New Ostium Merchants Guild|New Ostium Merchants Guild]].

## Objective

Acquire specified supplies, equipment, money, or components for Old Ostium.

## Stakes

- Old Ostium capability.
- Criminal exposure in New Ostium.
- Guild-party relations.
- Who gets to decide that concentrated wealth justifies theft.

## Likely Scenes

These are modular possibilities, not a fixed sequence.

1. Establish the concrete need and why ordinary purchase failed.
2. Investigate Guild holdings and security.
3. Discover at least one non-theft route: bargain, investment pitch, expose a hoard, or exchange a service.
4. Execute chosen plan.
5. Handle investigation and allocation of the acquired resources.

## Obstacles & Complications

- New Ostium has records and law.
- Stolen goods may be identifiable.
- Beneficiaries may disagree about distribution.

> [!danger] City Guard escalation
> Any illegal business in New Ostium is highly dangerous. The [[Factions/New Ostium City Guard|New Ostium City Guard]], commanded by [[Characters/NPCs/Aaron Fortisax|Aaron Fortisax]], is well funded, excellently armed, and formidable. The PCs should not fight it under any circumstances, especially at low levels. Explain the danger before commitment; outright combat can bring an organized response. Discovery still follows actual witnesses and evidence. Preserve options to bargain, expose coercion, seek lawful help, or withdraw.
