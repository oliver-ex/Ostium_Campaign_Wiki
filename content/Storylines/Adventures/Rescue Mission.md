# Rescue Mission

<!-- page-summary:start -->

A prepared search for Orla Meren at Old Ostium University, combining rescue with unsettling evidence about the Rot and the Heart.

<!-- page-summary:end -->


## Function in the Larger Arc

Horror/exploration side adventure that develops the cognitive nature of the Rot and seeds deeper Heart clues.

## Hook / Pressure

A New Ostium scholar, [[Characters/NPCs/Orla Meren|Orla Meren]], entered [[Locations/Old Ostium University|Old Ostium University]] and has not returned. The party is asked to bring her back, preferably alive.

## Objective

Find Orla and return her to safety if possible.

## Stakes

- Orla's life and mental autonomy.
- University records.
- Evidence about the Rot's unusual cognitive effects.

## Key NPCs

- [[Characters/NPCs/Orla Meren|Orla Meren]].
- Patron can be New Ostium academic/government contact when activated.

## Locations

- [[Locations/Old Ostium University|Old Ostium University]]

## Factions

- Potential involvement from [[Factions/New Ostium Government|New Ostium Government]]; otherwise primarily independent.

## Likely Scenes

These are modular possibilities, not a fixed sequence.

1. Briefing from worried colleagues who disagree about what Orla was studying.
2. Approach to the University through a Rot-heavy district.
3. Explore synchronized and psychosis-like phenomena without knowing which clues are trustworthy.
4. Locate Orla, who may be partly entangled with the collective behaviour and reluctant or unable to simply leave.
5. Extract her, stabilize her, or follow a clue deeper at significant cost.

## Obstacles & Complications

- Collective attention makes stealth/social approaches strange.
- Violence can turn many inhabitants against the party at once.
- Orla's own curiosity works against a simple rescue.

## Clues & Revelations

- The collective phenomena have a real connection to deeper cognitive influence.
- Orla can return with useful observations rather than just trauma.

## Meaningful Choices

- Prioritize Orla or records.
- Force her out or persuade her.
- Treat the inhabitants as victims, threats, or possible sources of knowledge.

## Resolutions

- Rescue should support multiple paths and should not require killing university inhabitants.

## Consequences

### If successful

- Orla becomes a research ally and deep-lore clue source.

### If partially successful

- Orla returns altered or some records are lost.

### If failed / abandoned

- Orla remains inside; the party still learns enough to advance the world state.

- Seeds Act II Heart/Rot explanation without fully revealing it.
