# Carl Marsh — Unsent Letter

> Tentative optional handout. Reveal only if discovered; retain the master here. Exact wording is proposed, not a new campaign event.

My love,

I keep starting this letter because I don't know how to tell you where the money is coming from. I've joined people who take what isn't theirs. I tell myself it's for food, and for her medicine. I know that doesn't make it right.

Please tell her I'm thinking of her. Don't promise I'll bring enough. I can't stand the thought of disappointing her again.

I wish there were somewhere I could ask for help without having to explain why we still live in Old Ostium. You shouldn't have to pay for my choices. Neither should she.

I'll try to come home.

Carl
