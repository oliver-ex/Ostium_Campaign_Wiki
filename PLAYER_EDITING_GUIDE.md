# Campaign editing guide — retained entry point

The wiki is now comprehensive and DM-facing. The former player/GM publishing split has been retired by explicit GM instruction. See [README.md](README.md) for editing and collaboration, and [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) for publication.

Player character identities and choices remain player-owned. GM collaborators must not invent or overwrite builds, backgrounds, or character motivations. Record actual PC discoveries separately from hidden GM context inside the same entity note.
